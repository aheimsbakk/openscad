Vase-mode C-channels and resting ledges require continuous perimeter folding with dedicated feature vertices rather than 1D normal displacement. The resting ledge forms via a forward-ramping shelf with a 45-degree chamfer transition in Z, avoiding mid-air horizontal bridges over the hollow interior while providing a positive stop for the slide-in plaque.
